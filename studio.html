i.

How a project runs

01

Brief & vision

A short call or voice note, then a one-page vision: goal, audience, formats, references and a fixed quote.

02

Look frames

Key frames and a mood for product, light and palette. Nothing moves until you approve the look.

03

Drafts & finals

Motion drafts to test the camera and timing, then final shots in full resolution.

04

Finish & deliver

Edit, sound, grade and every aspect ratio you need, with two rounds of revisions built in.

ii.

Principles

One vision, set on day one

Every project starts with a single sentence the film has to live up to, like "as premium and luxurious as possible". Each draft is judged against it, which keeps the work from drifting.

Drafts are cheap, finals are deliberate

Ideas are tested at low resolution first. Final renders only happen once a shot already works, which keeps quality high and budgets honest.

Sound is half the film

Every piece gets a considered score and sound design, mixed for phones and laptops, using royalty-free or original audio only.

Honest about AI

I say clearly what was made with AI and which brands are invented. Clients get full usage rights to the final deliverables within the licence terms of the tools used.
