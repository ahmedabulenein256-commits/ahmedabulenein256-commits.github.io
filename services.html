Questions

How long does a project take?

Most product films and motion promos are delivered in 5–10 working days from an approved brief. Story films take 2–3 weeks. Rush delivery is possible when the schedule allows.

Can you use my real product?

Yes. Send clear product photos from a few angles and I build the film around them, keeping shape, colour and logo faithful. Fine details are checked frame by frame.

Who owns the final video?

You do. You get full commercial usage rights to the final deliverables, within the licence terms of the AI tools used. Music and sound are royalty-free or original.

How do revisions work?

Two rounds are included at each approval stage: look frames, then the edit. Because you approve the look before motion, late surprises are rare.

How do I pay?

50% to start and 50% on delivery, by bank transfer or an international payment service. A fixed quote is agreed before any work begins.
