Questions

How long does a project take?

Spark takes 4 to 5 working days, Feature 7 to 10 working days and Signature 2 to 3 weeks, counted from the day you approve the brief and send your materials. Rush delivery takes about half that time and costs 50% extra.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, brand fonts and colours, product photos and any legal text that must appear, before work starts. If materials arrive late, the delivery date moves by the same number of days.

Can you use my real product?

Yes. Send clear product photos from a few angles and I build the film around them, keeping the shape, colour and logo faithful. Fine details are checked frame by frame.

How do revisions work?

Two rounds are included. You approve the concept or script first, then the cut. A round is one list of changes sent together. A new concept or script after approval is a new project.

Who owns the final video?

You do. Once the project is paid in full you can use the film commercially, in organic posts and paid ads, in any country. I keep the right to show it in my portfolio. Editable project files are not included and are quoted separately.

How do I pay?

50% to start. The other 50% is due after you approve a watermarked preview, and the clean files are sent once it is paid. Transfer fees are paid by the client. The deposit is not refunded once concept work has started.

Can I have a human voice actor?

Yes. The voice over in the packages is made with AI. A human voice actor costs the artist's fee plus 15%.
