Questions

The things clients ask before we start.

How fast can you deliver?

Short films are usually ready within days of the approved look. I confirm the delivery date in writing before we start.

Which languages do you work in?

Arabic and English, for both the on screen type and the voiceover. Arabic voices are available in Saudi, Emirati, Egyptian and Levantine accents.

What sizes do I receive?

Every size your launch needs, such as vertical for social, square for feeds and wide for web and screens.

Do I need to send footage or products?

No. Clear photos of the product, your logo and your brand colours are enough to start.

Have a launch coming up?

Tell me what you are launching and when. You get a plan and a delivery date back.
