VERDELLE Moonblade

A diamond necklace cut by moonlight. Monochrome, quiet and precise.

The brief

Starting from a single reference photo of a diamond necklace, the brief was premium, elegant and luxurious, with a creative concept and camera rather than a plain product showcase.

The approach

  1. Concept test. An early "Midnight Dew" direction was drafted and dropped when the drafts stopped improving.
  2. The Moonblade direction. Black and white, hard moonlight, the stones as the only highlights in frame.
  3. One final generation. Shot design locked in drafts, then rendered once at 1080p.
  4. Finish in code. Grade, sparkle control, grain and sound added after the picture was locked.

The result

A 25-second monochrome jewellery film. Knowing when to kill a concept saved the budget for the version that worked.
