LUMAVERA First Light

A one minute launch film for a waterfront tower in Dubai that does not exist yet. One gold line becomes a tower, and then a life.

Brand
Lumavera by Azalen Developments (invented)
Role
Concept, direction, motion graphics, edit, sound
Format
16:9 · 1080p · 60 fps
Runtime
1:00
Voice
Emirati Arabic voice-over · English captions

The brief

Off-plan homes are sold before they are built, so the film has to sell the life as well as the building. I set one test for it: a Dubai developer should believe it is a real launch film.

The approach

  1. One gold line. An architect's pen line becomes the sketch, the model, a wireframe of light on the empty site, and then the finished tower.
  2. Graphics inside the world. The name is set in light on the sand, labels are pinned to the facade, and a day tracker runs from dawn to night. Nothing sits on top as a flat overlay.
  3. One day at Lumavera. Dawn at the pool, coffee poured, a quiet living room, sunset on the marina, then night.
  4. Arabic voice, English text. Nine short lines of Emirati Arabic voice-over, with English on screen and captions in both languages.
  5. Built for 60 fps. The graphics are rendered at 60 frames per second, and the lifestyle shots are slowed so the pace stays calm.

The result

A sixty second film that takes a tower from a pen line to its first night, and closes on an end card styled like a real developer's. The first cut was scrapped as bland and rebuilt from launch films I studied, which is where the gold line came from.

About AI and rights. This is a concept film. Lumavera and Azalen Developments are invented names, the permit and escrow numbers on the end card are masked, and the QR code is a placeholder. Made with generative AI tools under my direction: concept, shot design, selection, edit, sound and finish are mine. Sound effects are real recordings, royalty-free.
