Dragon Song The Last March

A warrior and her dragon on one last march. A ground-up remake of my first fantasy short, rebuilt as a real piece of cinema.

The brief

The first Dragon Song proved the idea but not the craft. The goal for 2.0 was simple and hard: make an AI fantasy short that holds up next to a real trailer, with characters that stay the same from shot to shot, deliberate camera language and a score and sound mix built for the cut.

The approach

  1. World and character lock. 18 reference images to fix the warrior, the dragon, the armour and the light before a single frame moved.
  2. Cheap drafts first. 12 motion drafts at 480p to test performance, camera and continuity, so credits were only spent on shots that already worked.
  3. Hero shots at 1080p. 5 final generations with Seedance, chosen for scale, emotion and the story beat each one had to carry.
  4. Cut, score and sound. Edited to a Suno score; footsteps, wind, wings and roars layered in code, then graded to a 2.39:1 scope letterbox.

The result

An 89-second short that reads as one world: consistent faces, a clear beat structure (march, threat, bond, fire) and a mix where music leads and sound design lands on the cut. It now opens my showreel.
