The brief

A full luxury e-commerce front end for two of my invented brands, the ORVANE watches and the ASÉLYA fragrances, using the product films as hero content. Built as my course graduation project.

The approach

  1. Film-led homepage. The Blue Lotus film is the hero, with "Shop" and "Watch the film" side by side.
  2. Two houses, one boutique. Perfumes and watches each get their own look, sharing one elegant system.
  3. Real shop flows. Product pages with specifications, filters, a bag and a concierge entry point.
  4. Live and responsive. Published on GitHub Pages and designed for desktop and mobile.

The result

A live, clickable site that shows how product films work inside a real store, not just on social.
