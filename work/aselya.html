ASÉLYA Blue Lotus

A blue lotus opens on the Nile at midnight and reveals the bottle.

The brief

My first product commercial: a fragrance launch film for an invented Egyptian perfume house, built around one image people remember, the flower that opens to reveal the bottle.

The approach

  1. Story in one move. River, moon, lotus, bottle. Each shot pushes closer to the reveal.
  2. A palette of blues. The scene moved from dawn to a midnight Nile so the colour of the scent owns the frame.
  3. Glass that looks real. Reflections and visible liquid were the details that made the bottle believable.
  4. Finish. 4K master, measured saturation and a soft score.

The result

A 16-second hero film. ASÉLYA became the fragrance line on the Aurèle site, with this film as its homepage hero.
