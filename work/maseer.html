The brief

A sample made for a client pitch: a vertical promo for an app that books local experiences across Saudi Arabia, in Arabic, with a voice that sounds local, in a style tailored to the client.

The approach

  1. Paper-collage look. Torn paper, stamps and hand-cut photos for Abha's fog, AlUla's rocks and desert nights.
  2. Arabic typography. Right-to-left layouts, Arabic headlines on paper strips and real app screens inside a phone.
  3. A Saudi voice. Voice-over generated and directed for a natural Saudi accent and pacing.
  4. Smooth 60 fps. Built in code for crisp type and fluid motion on phones.

The result

A 37-second pitch sample (v1). It shows what I bring to Gulf brands: native Arabic motion design, local voice and a look built for the brand rather than a template.
